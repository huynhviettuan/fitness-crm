# 03 — Domain Model

## Customer

Represents one gym customer/member.

Key properties:

- `id` — database UUID
- `customerCode` — business ID, generated by the server, immutable and unique (T-019, A-003)
- `fullName`
- `phone`
- `email` (optional)
- `dateOfBirth` (optional)
- `status`
- notes

A customer can own multiple contracts.

## Contract

Represents one purchased training/service package.

Key properties:

- `contractCode`
- `customerId`
- `packageName`
- `contractValue`
- `totalSessions`
- `startDate`
- `endDate`
- `status` — stored as `DRAFT`, `ACTIVE`, or `CANCELLED`

Derived values:

- completed sessions — bookings of the contract with status `COMPLETED` (T-012)
- remaining sessions
- confirmed paid amount
- outstanding debt
- `EXPIRED` status — `ACTIVE` with `endDate` before today in the gym timezone (T-018)

## Trainer

Represents a PT/trainer who can receive bookings and complete sessions.

A trainer may be linked to one user account. A user with role `TRAINER` must be linked to exactly one trainer record (A-010).

## Booking

Represents a scheduled appointment between customer, contract, and trainer.

A booking is a schedule record, not proof that a service was delivered.

The booking owns the single lifecycle status (T-011):

- BOOKED
- CHECKED_IN
- COMPLETED
- CANCELLED
- NO_SHOW

## Session

Represents the operational fulfillment of a booking: proof that the customer arrived and the service was delivered.

A session record is created at check-in. It stores who checked the customer in and who completed the session, and when. It has no status of its own; "session status" elsewhere in these documents means the status of its booking.

## Payment

Represents a confirmed or voided payment associated with a contract.

A payment is created as `CONFIRMED`. It can be voided with a reason; a voided payment is never reinstated (A-008).

Only confirmed payments contribute to paid amount.

## Cash Transaction

Represents broader internal income or expense entries not necessarily identical to contract payments.

A cash transaction is corrected by voiding it and recording a new one (A-009).

## User / Role

Represents application access and authorization.

Each user has exactly one role from a fixed set (T-020) and a status of `ACTIVE` or `DISABLED`.

## Audit Event

Immutable record of a critical action, written in the same transaction as the action (T-022), such as:

- contract created/updated/cancelled
- payment created/voided
- booking status change
- session completed
- session completion correction (if allowed, see OQ-005)
